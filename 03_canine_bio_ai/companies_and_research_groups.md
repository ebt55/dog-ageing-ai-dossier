## TL;DR

- **The open-data backbone for a dog-ageing AI project is already large and mostly public**: Dog10K (1,987 canid genomes incl. 63 wolves, 52.96 M SNVs, free, no login) [1][2]; the Dog Aging Project (DAP; >50,000 dogs enrolled, annual curated releases on Broad's Terra platform, 976-dog multi-omic Precision Cohort) [3][4][5]; DoGA (tissue biobank of 132 tissues from 13 dogs, 12 embryos and 24 wolves; published STRT2-seq promoter atlas of 100 tissues from 9 dogs + 12 embryos, plus a 2026 CAGE enhancer/promoter preprint from 9 dogs — wolves not yet in a published atlas) [6][7] [verifier-corrected]; CanVAS (15,451 imputed dog genotypes, CC-BY) [8]; the Mammalian Methylation Consortium's dog arrays (742 dogs, 93 breeds; GEO) [9][10]. ✅
- **The biggest canine datasets are private**: Wisdom Panel/Mars (an existing cohort of **3.5 M** genotyped dogs by 2023, of which 1,054,293 were analysed in Donner et al. 2023; 1.2 M in a 2024 study) [12][13] [verifier-corrected: the author listed Wisdom Panel as ~1.1–1.2 M, implying it was smaller than Embark]; Embark (2.5 M dogs tested as of Apr 2025, 230 k markers) [11]; Mars Petcare Biobank (proprietary; "available on reasonable request") [14]. Mars's Broad partnership promises 10,000 dog + 10,000 cat genomes into NCBI SRA over 10 years (announced Jan 2023) [15]. ✅
- **The academic centre of gravity has shifted**: DAP founders are now at Optispan (Kaeberlein, CEO), Tufts (Promislow) and Texas A&M (Creevy) and run the Dog Aging Institute nonprofit (founded 2023) [16][17]; the epigenetics arm sits at Arizona State (Snyder-Mackler) [18]; DAP survived a 2024 NIH funding lapse (NIA U19 not renewed; funding ran out mid-2024) and won a $7 M NIH (NIA) grant (announced 6 Jan 2025) to expand the TRIAD rapamycin trial from 170 to 580 dogs [19][53]. ✅ [verifier-upgraded: lapse corroborated by STAT, WGBH and AVMA coverage]
- **Industry is converging on dogs from three directions**: (1) longevity drugs — Loyal (LOY-002 safety and efficacy sections accepted by FDA, Jan 2026; $100 M Series C, Feb 2026) [20][21]; (2) gene therapy — Rejuvenate Bio (RJB-01 for mitral valve disease; $6 M round led by VCapital with Merck Animal Health participating, plus an R&D collaboration, announced 2 Jun 2026) [22]; (3) de-extinction/cloning — Colossal (gray wolves with 20 edits in 14 genes, 2024–25; bought ViaGen Pets Nov 2025; $10.2 B valuation Jan 2025, with talks reported in Jul–Sep 2026 for a new round at $20–30 B, not closed as of the reports) [23]–[27][71]. ✅/🟡 [verifier-corrected/added]
- **Colossal's "dire wolves" are the only published-in-public canid germline-editing project of the 2020s**, but the peer-reviewed record is only the paleogenome/ancestry paper (Gedman … Shapiro, bioRxiv Apr 2025, **published in *Cell Genomics* 6(8):101306, Aug 2026**, doi:10.1016/j.xgen.2026.101306) [25] [verifier-corrected: author wrote "no journal version as of Oct 2026"]; there is still no peer-reviewed methods paper for the edited animals. The project has drawn formal rebuttals (Jensen-Seaman et al., *Anat Rec* 2026) and criticism from Rawlence, Austin, Mech, Boitani and the IUCN SSC [25][26][27]. ✅/🟡 China's first CRISPR dogs (MSTN-knockout beagles Hercules & Tiangou, 2015) came from GIBH/CAS, not Sinogene; Sinogene is a cloning company (first cloned Arctic wolf, 2022) [28][29]. ✅/🟡
- **AI-native players in dog health are mostly diagnostic**: Zoetis (Vetscan Imagyst AI; Basepaws acquisition 2022) [30], Antech/Mars (RenalTech, cats) [31], SignalPET, FidoCure/Stanford (2,119-dog clinico-genomic survival analysis — Cox regression, not ML) [32] [verifier-corrected], SAVSNET Liverpool (PetBERT) [33], Stanford Zou lab (VetLLM) [34]. None is yet an ageing-focused AI company; Fauna Bio does comparative-genomics AI but across 400+ mammals, not dogs [35]. ✅/🟡
- **Wolf side**: vonHoldt (Princeton; Yellowstone wolves; co-author of the first dog+wolf epigenetic clock and a 25-wolf ageing-transcriptome study) [36][37], the Skoglund/Bergström ancient-wolf genomics group (72 ancient wolf genomes, Nature 2022) [38], and Dog10K's 63 wolf genomes (plus DoGA's 24 biobanked wolves, not yet profiled in a published atlas [verifier-corrected]) are the realistic wolf-data sources. Robert Wayne (UCLA) died Dec 2022 [39]. ✅/🟡

## 2. Profiles

### 2.1 Longitudinal cohorts and open data

**Dog Aging Project (DAP)** — Founded 2014 at the University of Washington by Daniel Promislow and Matt Kaeberlein (both then UW) with Kate Creevy (veterinary internist, then and now at Texas A&M; DAP Chief Veterinary Officer) [verifier-corrected: Creevy was not at UW]; funded by NIA U19 AG057377 plus donations [3][52] ✅. Enrollment >50,000 companion dogs (DAP/press, 🟡) with ~40,000 registered by 2022 [52] 🟡. Core components: Health and Life Experience Survey (200+ questions), EMR capture, the **Precision Cohort** (1,000 dog–owner pairs; 976 dogs sampled for CBC/chemistry, flow immunophenotype, metabolomics, faecal microbiome, epigenome; *GeroScience* 2025) [5] ✅, and **TRIAD** (Test of Rapamycin In Aging Dogs; expanding from 170 to 580 dogs, 3-year follow-up with 6-monthly visits; the release cites 20 participating locations and lists 17 named sites; led by Creevy with May Reed (UW) and Rozalyn Anderson (Wisconsin); $7 M NIH grant announced 6 Jan 2025) [19] ✅. Funding history: the NIA U19 was not renewed and ran out in mid-2024, forcing a fundraising push (target reported as $40–50 M) [53] ✅ (STAT headline plus WGBH/AVMA coverage) [verifier-upgraded]; the Dog Aging Institute (incorporated 6 Dec 2023) was created to bridge [16] ✅. **Data access**: de-identified curated data released annually (2023, 2024, 2025 releases) via Terra at the Broad Institute after application at dogagingproject.org/data-access; summary tables at data.dogagingproject.org [4] ✅. Published AI/stat outputs include comorbidity networks on 26,614 dogs [54], shared dog–human mortality biomarkers from 937 Precision-Cohort dogs [55], and an epigenetic-clock preprint on 864 dogs linking breed size to accelerated epigenetic ageing and mortality [18] ✅. **Collaborator value: very high** (only dataset combining tens of thousands of phenotyped dogs with multi-omics and an RCT).

**Dog Aging Institute** — 501(c)(3), incorporated 6 Dec 2023 (FAQ) [verifier-added date]; legally separate from DAP, which cannot accept donations directly; board: Kaeberlein (President; CEO of Optispan), Creevy (Secretary; Professor, Texas A&M CVM), Promislow (Treasurer & Chair; Senior Scientist and Scientific Advisor, Tufts) [17] ✅.

**Morris Animal Foundation Golden Retriever Lifetime Study (GRLS)** — 3,044 US goldens aged 6 mo–2 y enrolled June 2012–April 2015, followed for life with annual biospecimens; primary endpoints hemangiosarcoma, osteosarcoma, lymphoma, high-grade mast cell tumour (Labadie et al., *PLoS One* 2022) [45] ✅. Reported 500 cumulative diagnoses of the four cancers; 34 external researchers granted data/sample access; six new studies and nine publications in 2025 (foundation web pages, could not be opened directly) [46] 🟡. **Collaborator value: high for cancer-ageing in one breed**; access is proposal-based and researcher-funded 🟡.

**Mars Petcare Biobank** — launched 2022; target up to 20,000 dogs and cats over 10 years; >4,000 enrolled (Mars page, 🟡) [13]; first-2,000-dog cohort profile in *BMC Vet Res* 2026 reports annual haematology/biochemistry banking and owner QoL/diet data, with datasets "proprietary but available … on reasonable request" [14] ✅. Jan 2023 (9 Jan) Mars–Broad agreement: sequence 10,000 dog + 10,000 cat genomes over 10 years (Broad, led by Elinor Karlsson), deposit in NCBI SRA, first raw data promised "throughout 2023" [15] ✅ — a verifier ENA search for Mars/Biobank-titled dog studies found none, so actual deposits remain unconfirmed ❓.

**Darwin's Ark** — Elinor Karlsson (UMass Chan / Broad); community-science platform with ~46,000 dogs and 14,000 cats enrolled and ~5,000 dogs sequenced; Darwin's Cats launched 2024 [44] 🟡 (The Scientist). Key paper: Morrill K., … Karlsson E.K. (2022) *Science*, "Ancestry-inclusive dog genomics challenges popular breed stereotypes" — 18,385 purebred and mixed-breed dogs surveyed, 2,155 genotyped; "breed explains just 9% of behavioral variation in individuals" although most behavioural traits are heritable (h² > 25 %) [56] ✅ (abstract via Europe PMC). **Collaborator value: medium-high** for behaviour/phenotype-rich cohorts; data via collaboration rather than download 🟡.

**OLD DOG (Italy)** — Zemko, Bonsembiante, Canevelli … Banzato (Padua, Sapienza, Milan): 30-month prospective study of 209 dogs showing consistent age patterns in haematology, inflammation, physical and cognitive measures (*J Frailty Aging* 2026) [49] ✅ (abstract).

### 2.2 Genomics consortia and reference resources

**Dog10K** — Led from Kunming Institute of Zoology (Guo-Dong Wang, Ya-Ping Zhang) with Uppsala (Meadows), Michigan (Kidd), NHGRI (Ostrander) and others. Genome Biology 2023 (24:187): 1,987 individuals — 1,611 dogs (321 breeds), 309 village dogs, 63 wolves, 4 coyotes; >48 M SNVs, indels and SVs [1] ✅. Database (NAR 2025, 53:D939–D947): 52,963,969 SNVs per Table 1 (the same paper's text says 34.6 M — an internal inconsistency in the source [verifier-added]), 8,565 de novo mutations (643 dogs, 404 trios), snRNA-seq hippocampus (105,057 nuclei), scRNA-seq leukocytes (74,067 cells), an ageing-transcriptome comparison of 30 dogs vs 469 humans, and an "AgeConversion" dog↔human age tool; free, no registration [2] ✅. **Collaborator value: essential** reference panel.

**NHGRI Dog Genome Project** — Elaine Ostrander, Chief & NIH Distinguished Investigator, Cancer Genetics and Comparative Genomics Branch [42] ✅; foci: morphology, breed structure, canine and prostate cancer; co-author on Dog10K and the dog methylation co-analysis [40]; public data release page [41] ✅. No retirement/lab-closure information found ✅.

**DoGA (Dog Genome Annotation) Consortium** — Hannes Lohi (Helsinki) & Carsten Daub (Karolinska); launched 2017; main support from the Jane and Aatos Erkko Foundation (site, 🟡) [43]. *Nat Commun* 15:9082 (2024): biobank of 5,485 samples, 132 tissues, 13 dogs, 12 embryos, 24 wolves; the published promoter/expression atlas used **STRT2-seq** on 100 tissues from 9 dogs + 12 embryos (no wolves) [6] ✅ [verifier-corrected: author wrote "5,485 CAGE samples … 24 wolves"]; 2026 preprint: 68,446 promoters, 46,661 enhancers from 114 **CAGE** libraries/56 tissues & stages/9 dogs [7] ✅. Data via DoGA DCC and GitLab; prepublication access by emailing Lohi [43] ✅. **Collaborator value: high** — the most comprehensive canine transcription-defined regulatory atlas, and the only one with a matched wolf tissue biobank (wolf profiles not yet published) [verifier-corrected].

**CanVAS** — David Brundage (UW–Madison SVM), bioRxiv Apr 2026: 15 public datasets → 15,451 dogs (375+ breeds, village dogs, dingoes, wolves, coyotes), CanFam4, imputed against Dog10K (1,929 dogs) → 9.7 M variants; Zenodo record 19186944 (published 23 Mar 2026), CC-BY 4.0 — licence confirmed via the Zenodo API [8] ✅.

**Wolf genomics** — (i) Bergström, Skoglund et al. 2022 *Nature* 607:313–320: 72 ancient wolf genomes spanning 100,000 years; dual wolf ancestry of dogs [38] ✅ (abstract) [verifier-upgraded]. (ii) **vonHoldt lab (Princeton)**: Yellowstone wolf population genomics (76,859 loci, 400+ wolves) 🟡 [36]; co-author of the 2017 dog+wolf epigenetic clock (46 dogs, 62 wolves) [37] ✅ and of Charruau et al. 2016 *MBE* — blood transcriptomes of 25 Yellowstone wolves: 625 age-associated genes, immunosenescence signatures mirroring humans but on a years-not-decades timescale [57] 🟡 [verifier-downgraded: the abstract confirms whole-blood RNA-seq of wild Yellowstone gray wolves with conserved immunosenescence/metabolic ageing signatures, but the 25-wolf and 625-gene figures could not be checked (no open full text)]. (iii) **Robert K. Wayne (UCLA)**, founder of canid conservation genetics, died 26 Dec 2022 aged 66 [39] 🟡. (iv) Wolves in open datasets: Dog10K (63), DoGA (24 in the biobank; not in the published atlas), CanVAS (incl. wolves), Colossal's two dire-wolf paleogenomes (*Cell Genomics* 2026) [1][6][8][25] [verifier-corrected].

### 2.3 Companies: consumer genomics, diagnostics, nutrition

**Embark Veterinary** — co-founded 2015 by Adam and Ryan Boyko; Cornell research partnership; database of 2.5 M dogs; >230,000 markers; 270+ health risks, 350 breeds, 55 traits [11] ✅ (Cornell news, Apr 2025 — figures may have grown since). Note that Wisdom Panel's 2023 cohort (3.5 M dogs) was larger [12] [verifier-added]. **Age Test** (launched 4 Oct 2022, $159): DNA-methylation algorithm; claims 70 % of dogs within ±5 months, 92 % within ±10 months [47] ✅ (company press release — no peer-reviewed validation found 🟡). Help-centre text says the algorithm was built from >2,000 dogs of known birthdate 🟡 [58]. **Collaborator value: very high but gated** — largest genotype+phenotype resource; academic collaborations exist (Cornell pannus, sled-dog genomics) [11].

**Mars Petcare / Wisdom Panel / Kinship** — Donner et al. 2023 *PLoS Genet* 19(2):e1010651: Mendelian disease-variant patterns in 1,054,293 dogs (811,628 mixed-breed, 242,665 purebred) drawn from an existing Wisdom Panel cohort of **3.5 million** dogs; variant load rises as heterozygosity falls [12] ✅ [verifier-added cohort size]. A 2024 1.2 M-dog study (Wisdom Panel + Banfield + Biobank) found a *SLAMF1* variant in 71 % of French Bulldogs and 40 % of Boxers doubling atopic-dermatitis risk [13] 🟡 (company page). **Antech Diagnostics (Mars) RenalTech**: ML model on ~150,000 Banfield cats predicting CKD up to 2 years early (>95 % accuracy claimed) — feline only [31] 🟡; RapidRead Dental AI (May 2025) 🟡.

**Zoetis** — FY2025 10-K: Vetscan Imagyst gained AI dermatology and AI fecal for equine (2023) and AI urine sediment (2024); the separate Vetscan OptiCell AI haematology analyser launched in 2024 [verifier-refined]; "AI Masses … detects potentially neoplastic cells" (2025); acquired Basepaws (pet genetics, early health-risk assessment) in 2022 [30] ✅. No ageing-specific programme found ✅.

**Purina Institute / Nestlé Purina; Hill's Pet Nutrition** — Purina: medium-chain-triglyceride diets improve cognitive-test performance in senior dogs (brand research + *Br J Nutr* paper) [50] 🟡; Hill's: antioxidant-enriched diets + enrichment improve cognition in ageing dogs (Prescription Diet b/d literature) [51] 🟡. Relevant as diet-intervention partners; no AI/genomics programme found ✅.

### 2.4 Longevity and intervention companies (summary only — details in ../05_interventions/)

**Loyal (Cellular Longevity Inc., San Francisco)** — Programmes LOY-001 (large/giant-breed injectable), LOY-002 (daily pill for senior dogs), LOY-003 (daily pill for large dogs; FDA accepted its RXE section, announced 16 Sep 2026 via BusinessWire — the third RXE across Loyal's portfolio) [verifier-added; 🟡 search summary of the press release]. FDA CVM accepted LOY-002's Reasonable Expectation of Effectiveness (Feb 2025) and Target Animal Safety (13 Jan 2026; >400 treated dogs) sections of the Expanded Conditional Approval application — manufacturing (CMC) remains; the STAY pivotal trial is fully enrolled at 1,300+ dogs across 70+ clinics [20][21] ✅. $100 M Series C led by age1 with The Longevity Fund, Baillie Gifford and existing investors (11 Feb 2026) [21] ✅; total raised >$250 M — not stated in the Series C post, unverified ❓ [verifier-downgraded]. As of early Oct 2026 no FDA acceptance of the CMC section or conditional approval was found (company/press guidance points to late 2026–2027) 🟡 [verifier-added]. **Relevance**: first company likely to market a lifespan-extension claim in any species; no public data.

**Rejuvenate Bio (San Diego; George Church lab spinout)** — RJB-01: AAV gene therapy co-expressing FGF21 and sTGFβR2 for canine myxomatous mitral valve disease; pilot data in Cavalier King Charles Spaniels (ASGCT 2023) 🟡 [59]; Phibro partnership announced 27 Jun 2022 with a stated aim to file for conditional approval "as early as 2023" (not found to have happened) [60] ✅; 2 Jun 2026 (BusinessWire; PR Newswire re-release by investor Lifespan Vision Ventures on 11 Jun 2026): $6 M financing led by VCapital with Merck Animal Health, Kendall Capital Partners, Connecticut Innovations and Digitalis, plus a Merck Animal Health R&D collaboration on "a novel gene therapy program in animal health" [22] ✅ [verifier-corrected date and upgraded — no longer single-source]; the second asset RJB-0402 (liver-directed FGF21) appears only in the PackGene write-up 🟡.

**Fauna Bio** — "Convergence" comparative-genomics AI platform across 452 mammals (65 hibernators); Lilly obesity collaboration (21 Dec 2023, up to $494 M milestones) [35] 🟡; Novo Nordisk collaboration 🟡; first candidate targets HFpEF 🟡. Not dog-focused; relevant as a template for cross-species AI target discovery.

### 2.5 Gene editing and cloning in canids

**Colossal Biosciences (Dallas)** — founded 2021 by George Church and Ben Lamm; $200 M Series C (Jan 2025) at $10.2 B valuation; $435 M raised in total as of Jan 2025 [23] 🟡 (Wikipedia; TechCrunch Jan 2025). [verifier-added] TechCrunch (20 Jul 2026) and Axios (30 Sep 2026) report talks to raise a new round at roughly $20–30 B, reportedly led by TWG Global; no closing announced in those reports [71] 🟡. **"Dire wolf" project (company claims)**: three gray wolves — Romulus & Remus born 1 Oct 2024, Khaleesi 30 Jan 2025 — carrying 20 edits in 14 genes; edits made by CRISPR in endothelial progenitor cells isolated from gray-wolf blood, nuclei transferred by SCNT into denucleated ova, embryos carried by domestic-dog surrogates and delivered by caesarean [26][27] 🟡. Only 15 of the 20 edits derive from dire-wolf sequence; 5 are dog/wolf coat-colour variants (MC1R, MFSD12) [26] 🟡. Colossal's own project page gives no edit counts, methods or publications [61] ✅. **Scientific record**: Gedman, Morrill Pirovich, Oppenheimer … Shapiro, bioRxiv 11 Apr 2025 (CC BY-NC-ND) — two dire-wolf paleogenomes (3.4× and 12.8×; >13 kyr and >72 kyr), 10 canid species compared, ~2/3 of dire-wolf ancestry sister to the wolf/coyote/dhole clade, 80 genes under diversifying selection (preprint figures, not re-checked against the journal version 🟡); **published as "On the ancestry and evolution of the extinct dire wolf", *Cell Genomics* 6(8):101306 (Aug 2026), doi:10.1016/j.xgen.2026.101306** (bioRxiv API "published" field + Crossref) [25] ✅ [verifier-corrected: author wrote "no 'now published in' link as of Oct 2026"]. This is a paleogenomics paper, not a methods paper for the edited animals. **Criticism**: Rawlence ("still very much a grey wolf"), Austin ("not a dire wolf under any definition"), Mech, Boitani; IUCN SSC said the project "does not contribute to conservation"; Jensen-Seaman, Hartstone-Rose, Burrows & Smith, "The dire wolf (*Aenocyon dirus*) resurrection that wasn't", *Anat Rec* 309(7):1788–1796 (online Sep 2025) [26][27] ✅/🟡. **Also**: four cloned red wolves (Hope, Blaze, Cinder, Ash) via EPC cloning, Apr 2025 🟡 [62]; acquired **ViaGen Pets & Equine** 4 Nov 2025 (described by Fast Company/D CEO as Colossal's first acquisition — though Wikipedia also lists an Aug 2025 acquisition of the University of Melbourne TIGRR lab; terms undisclosed; 25 staff stay in Texas) [24] ✅ [verifier-upgraded]; **25 Jun 2026 MOU with the US Department of the Interior / Fish and Wildlife Service** for a "BioVault" biobank of ~2,300 ESA-listed species, with no federal funds obligated [72] ✅ [verifier-upgraded]. **Relevance to dog ageing**: demonstrates that multiplex germline editing + SCNT in *Canis lupus* with dog surrogates is operational at a company scale — the enabling technology for any future "edit dog ageing genes" programme — but with no peer-reviewed methods paper for the edited animals ✅ (the 2026 *Cell Genomics* paper covers ancestry only) [verifier-clarified].

**GIBH/CAS (Guangzhou) — first gene-edited dogs** — Zou Q., Wang X., Liu Y. et al. (2015) *J Mol Cell Biol* 7:580–583: Cas9 mRNA + MSTN sgRNA injected into zygotes, auto-embryo transfer; 35 zygotes → 27 pups; two (Tiangou, biallelic; Hercules, mosaic) carried MSTN mutations [28] ✅. Institutions: CAS Key Laboratory of Regenerative Biology (GIBH, Guangzhou), Key Laboratory of Model Animal for Disease Study (Nanjing University), Guangzhou General Pharmaceutical Research Institute Co., Ltd, and Jilin University [28] ✅ — i.e., **not Sinogene** (Sinogene is not mentioned in the paper) [verifier-confirmed; added the commercial co-affiliation]. Note: 35 injected zygotes were transferred into 10 recipients; Hercules is described as chimeric (mosaic) ✅.

**Sinogene Biotechnology (Beijing)** — commercial pet cloning since 2017 (dogs), 2019 (cats), horses 2023; cloned "Longlong" (2017) from a gene-edited beagle "Apple" bred as an atherosclerosis model 🟡 [63]; first cloned Arctic wolf "Maya" born 2022 to a beagle surrogate; prices from ~$55,000; CEO Mi Jidong [29] 🟡.

**ViaGen Pets & Equine (Cedar Park, TX)** — founded 2002; pets since 2014–2016 (sources differ 🟡); SCNT cloning, ~$50,000 per dog, $25 k deposit, 5–7-month waitlist 🟡; 15 species cloned incl. black-footed ferret and Przewalski's horse; now a Colossal division [24][64] 🟡.
