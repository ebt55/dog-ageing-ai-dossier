# Verification process

> Part of the dog-ageing research dossier · 2026-10-04 · Licence: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Every research file in this dossier went through two passes:

1. **Research pass.** A subagent (Opus 5.5 or Fable 5.1) researched one topic on the web, preferring primary sources (papers, preprints, official docs, regulatory documents) and wrote the file. It tagged its key claims:
   - ✅ confirmed in a primary source the agent actually opened
   - 🟡 taken from an abstract or a reputable secondary source
   - ❓ unconfirmed, contested, or from recollection
2. **Independent verification pass.** A second subagent running on the *other* model family audited the file:
   - Opus-written files were checked by Fable, and Fable-written files by Opus.
   - The verifier re-checked the highest-impact and least-certain claims against primary sources.
   - It corrected errors in place and logged every change.
   - Its report is saved in this folder as `<topic>_verification.md`.

Each report lists the claims checked, the verdict on each (confirmed / corrected / downgraded / removed), and anything that is still uncertain.

## How to read confidence in this dossier

- A claim tagged ✅ that a verifier also confirmed is the strongest evidence here.
- 🟡 and ❓ claims are leads to follow up, not settled facts.
- Research proposals are labelled **PROPOSAL** and are speculative by design.
