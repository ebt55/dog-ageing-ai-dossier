# Canine Data Resources for AI Work on Dog & Canid Ageing

> Part of the dog-ageing research dossier · Written 2026-10-04 by Opus 5.5 subagent · Status: independently verified by Fable 5.1 on 2026-10-04 — see ../07_verification/ai_toolkit_verification.md · Licence: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## TL;DR

- **The single most valuable ageing-specific resource is the Dog Aging Project (DAP)**: ~47,400 dog–owner pairs in the "Pack" 🟡 (the exact 47,408 figure is not in the abstract I could read) [verifier-corrected], a deeply phenotyped **Precision Cohort of 976 dogs** (target 1,000) with blood/urine/faeces/hair biospecimens profiled by clinical pathology, flow-cytometry immunophenotyping, LC-MS metabolomics, **RRBS epigenomics** and faecal shotgun metagenomics ✅ (all assays verbatim in the Prescott 2025 abstract), plus **7,627 dogs with imputed low-pass genomes** ✅ — released annually and de-identified; the **Terra** distribution route and the one-year embargo are 🟡/❓ (DAP's own data-access page could not be read; Terra at the Broad is only confirmed as the analysis platform acknowledged by DAP papers) [verifier-corrected] [1][2][3][4].
- **Genomic breadth** comes from **Dog10K** (1,987 canids: 1,611 breed dogs/321 breeds, 309 village dogs, 63 wolves, 4 coyotes; >48M variants, annotated with Zoonomia phyloP) ✅ (every figure verbatim in the Meadows 2023 abstract) [5][6][7] and **Darwin's Ark** (18,385 surveys, 2,155 genotyped dogs) ✅ [8]. **Number reconciliation** [verifier-added]: ">48M" counts SNVs + indels + SVs together (Genome Biology); the Dog10K *database* paper's Table 1 gives **52,963,969 SNVs** while that same paper's text says "34.6 million SNVs" — cite the figure matching the file you download.
- **Longevity/mortality phenotypes at population scale** come from veterinary EHRs: **VetCompass** (≈7.5M UK dogs ❓ **unverified** — see §1.4; rvc.ac.uk blocks automated fetches and published VetCompass studies use denominators of ~1.5–2.4M dogs) [verifier-downgraded] and its life tables (30,563 deaths; life expectancy 11.23 y; French Bulldog 4.53 y vs Jack Russell 12.72 y) ✅ [9][10][11], plus the **Golden Retriever Lifetime Study** (3,044 goldens since 2012 ✅; >51M data points 🟡; genotypes for 3,224 dogs openly on AWS under CC-BY-SA ✅ — AWS registry confirms ~1.1M markers, 3,024 GRLS + 200 Golden Oldies, static, no AWS account needed) [12][13][14].
- **Reference genomes**: the Ensembl/UniProt reference is **ROS_Cfam_1.0** (Labrador, GCA_014441545.1) ✅ [15][16]; **UU_Cfam_GSD_1.0 / canFam4** (GCA_011100685.1) is the Dog10K variant-calling reference (strictly: UU_Cfam_GSD_1.0 **plus three chrY contigs from ROS_Cfam_1.0** [verifier-added]) and the one used by most new single-cell work [5][17]; 2024–2026 added a **near-T2T Beagle** (❓ figshare-only, no literature record found [verifier-downgraded]), **five diploid dual assemblies** (incl. Greenland wolf) ✅, and a **gapless Korean Jindo (TTAGGA)** assembly with a 21.3 Mb chrY ✅ [18][19][20].
- **Known gaps that constrain AI work**: dog is **absent from AlphaFold DB's bulk model-organism proteome downloads** (though individual dog entries exist) [21][22]; dog is **absent from the CELLxGENE Census** and from TranscriptFormer's trained/embedded species list [23][24]; there is **no canine equivalent of AlphaMissense/AlphaGenome precomputed scores**; and canine single-cell data are tissue-fragmented (no true pan-tissue "Canine Cell Atlas" was found).
- **Dog *is* in Evo 2's training corpus** (OpenGenome2 contains the dog assembly GCF_011100685.1 plus Ensembl r112 dog mRNA (26,040 transcripts) and ncRNA sets, and ncRNA sets for four breeds — basenji, boxer, great dane, GSD — plus dingo) — ✅ **independently re-verified**: I re-downloaded `species_metadata.csv` (76,071 rows) and reproduced every row [verifier-corrected: the original said "five breed ncRNA sets" but listed four; the fifth *Canis* ncRNA entry is the dingo, and a sixth is *C. l. familiaris* itself] [25]. Note that OpenGenome2 is **not Evo 2's alone**: InstaDeep's **NTv3** was also pretrained on 9 Tbp of OpenGenome2, so dog sequence is in its training data too [verifier-added].

## 1. Ageing-specific cohorts with biospecimens

### 1.1 Dog Aging Project (DAP) — the flagship ✅

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

**DAP-derived datasets/analyses worth reusing (each a potential AI training target):**
- **Epigenetic clock on 864 dogs**, >3M CpG sites, some sampled annually for 2–3 years: largest size class is on average **0.37 years epigenetically older per chronological year** than the smallest, and each year of residual epigenetic age carries a **34% higher mortality hazard** (HR 1.34, 95% CI 1.06–1.69) 🟡 (preprint) [29]. [verifier-added] **Still unpublished as of 2026-10-04** (bioRxiv v2, 29 Oct 2024; `published: NA`; no journal version in Europe PMC), and the mortality estimate rests on only **49 deaths** — do not cite it as established.
- **DNAm + ATAC-seq clocks on 71 dogs** (PBMCs): R² = 0.33 (DNAm), 0.26 (ATAC), 0.29 (combined); clock features near *BAIAP2* and *SCARF2* ✅ [30].
- **Plasma metabolome mortality model in 937 Precision-Cohort dogs**: metabolite→mortality effects correlate strongly with human all-cause-mortality metabolite effects ✅ [31].
- **Protein catabolites (post-translationally modified amino acids) as age biomarkers**; ~36% of measured metabolites show age effects; kidney function mediates ~half the ptmAA–age effect ✅ [3].
- **Gut microbiome of >900 dogs** (faecal shotgun metagenomics) with a metagenomics-based **population-level canine age predictor**, compared against the human Lifelines-DEEP cohort ✅ [32].
- **First canine GWAS catalogue**: 7,627 genotyped dogs, 976 with 159 blood metabolites/analytes; blood traits map to loci orthologous to human ones; globulin and potassium are elevated in short-lived breeds and predict individual mortality; ethanolamine is enriched in long-lived breeds 🟡 (preprint; every figure confirmed verbatim in the v2 abstract and full text [verifier-upgraded within 🟡]) [4]. [verifier-added] Scale: **298 genome-wide significant loci** (189 trait-level associations; 82 of 159 traits have ≥1 hit), and the mortality analysis uses **957 dogs with three years of follow-up, of which 11% died** (~105 deaths). The paper also notes that while some ageing-associated traits relate to growth-hormone pathways, **many do not** — "aging in dogs is multifactorial".
- **Breed-inference ML framework** on 6,572 DAP dogs across 100 breed classes (91.7% accuracy; ~150 SNPs suffice) 🟡 (preprint) [33].
- **Companion-dog Alzheimer's platform** with post-mortem protocols, built inside DAP ✅ [34].
- **EMR-vs-owner-report diagnosis accuracy study** — essential for anyone modelling DAP disease labels ✅ [35].

### 1.2 Golden Retriever Lifetime Study (GRLS) + Morris Animal Foundation Data Commons ✅

- **3,044 golden retrievers** followed since 2012; annual owner and veterinary questionnaires, physical exams, labs, biospecimen repository ("Bette M. Morris Data Commons & Biorepository") [12][36].
- Data Commons: **>51 million data points**, 11 subject areas (activity, behaviour, dental, disease diagnoses, environment, grooming, geography, lab results, medications, physical exams, reproduction); free to academic/nonprofit/government-affiliated researchers with approved credentials; commercial users must contact the Foundation [12].
- **Open genotypes**: AWS Open Data registry dataset `maf-genome` — **~1.1 million markers on 3,224 dogs** (3,024 main cohort + 200 "Golden Oldies"), Axiom Canine Genotyping Array Sets A & B, **CC-BY-SA**, `s3://mafgrlsgenome` (us-west-2), no AWS account required, static [13][14].
- Ageing relevance: single-breed design removes between-breed confounding; long follow-up with cause-of-death/cancer endpoints makes it the best canine survival-GWAS complement to DAP.

### 1.3 Darwin's Ark ✅

- Community-science platform; Morrill et al. 2022: **18,385 surveyed dogs**, **2,155 genotyped** (1,715 by low-pass sequencing at 0.6× ± 0.3×, 440 on an Axiom array); behaviour heritability h² > 25% for most traits; breed explains ~9% of behavioural variance [8][37].
- Lord et al. 2025 PNAS used **>3,000 Darwin's Ark dogs** with both genotypes and individual-level phenotypes; found commercial behaviour-variant tests have no predictive power but strong associations with aesthetic traits (height, leg length, ear shape) ✅ [38].
- Access: platform at darwinsark.org; open-data ethos is stated in the papers ❓ **Still unverified after an independent attempt** [verifier note]: `darwinsark.org/faq` is a 404 — the live paths are **`/faqs`** and **`/collaborate` ("Data Sharing & Collaboration")** [verifier-corrected] — and neither the accession numbers nor a licence for Darwin's Ark genomic data could be read. Treat Darwin's Ark as "collaboration/registration required until proven otherwise".
- Ageing relevance: indirect (phenotype breadth, mixed-breed ancestry mosaics useful for ancestry-aware ageing models), not an ageing cohort per se.

### 1.4 Veterinary primary-care EHR resources (lifespan/mortality at scale) ✅/🟡

- **VetCompass (RVC)**: de-identified EHR from **>1,800 UK practices (>30% of UK practices)** covering **>28 million companion/equine animals**, including **~7.5 million UK dogs**; fields include VeNom-coded diagnoses, free-text clinical notes (>200 million), treatments (>half a billion), DOB, sex, breed, insurance status, partial postcode ❓ **UNRESOLVED** [verifier-downgraded from 🟡]: rvc.ac.uk returned HTTP 403 to my fetches too (both `/vetcompass` and `/vetcompass/papers-and-data/open-access-data`), so **none of these numbers rests on a source either agent has opened.** For calibration, peer-reviewed VetCompass studies I could read use study denominators of **~1.5M dogs** (brain-tumour study, 2026) and **"over 2 million dogs"** (2019 demography, as cited in a 2026 review) — i.e. the 7.5M figure is presumably a cumulative all-time record count, not a study-ready cohort, and should not be quoted as the usable n [9].
- Access: collaboration/application via RVC; VetCompass also publishes an "Open Access data" page with per-study datasets 🟡 — **licence terms unverified (403 on both attempts)**.
- **Life tables** (Teng et al. 2022, *Sci Rep*): **30,563 dogs** that died 1 Jan 2016–31 Jul 2020; life expectancy at age 0 = **11.23 y** (95% CI 11.19–11.27); females 11.41 y vs males 11.07 y; **Jack Russell Terrier 12.72 y** (longest), **French Bulldog 4.53 y** (shortest); 18 breeds covered 50.6% of the population ✅ [verifier-confirmed: every figure verbatim in the abstract and full text] [10].
- **Breed longevity across 584,734 UK dogs** (incl. 284,734 deceased), 155 breeds, by size/sex/cephalic index, with phylogenetic clade partitioning (McMillan et al. 2024, *Sci Rep*) ✅ [11].
- Ageing relevance: the only datasets large enough to fit breed- and conformation-stratified survival models, and the natural validation layer for any molecular ageing-rate predictor.

### 1.5 Commercial direct-to-consumer cohorts (Wisdom Panel, Embark) 🟡

- **Wisdom Panel / Donner et al. 2023 (*PLoS Genetics*)**: **1,054,293 dogs** screened (from an existing cohort of 3.5 million) — 811,628 mixed-breed and 242,665 purebred, >150 countries — for **250 disease-associated variants**; veterinary EMR available for **43.5%**; **57% of dogs carry ≥1** studied Mendelian disease variant; full penetrance evidenced for 10 variants ✅ [39].
- **Embark** appears in the literature mainly as a collaborator/genotyping provider (e.g. guide-dog behaviour prediction, Dobermann DCM cross-population evaluation) rather than as an open dataset 🟡 [40][41].
- Access: **no open individual-level release found** for either company; aggregate allele frequencies are in paper supplements ❓. Treat as "commercial, collaboration-only".
- Ageing relevance: unmatched n for rare-variant frequency priors and for constructing breed-specific variant panels; weak longitudinal ageing phenotypes.

## 2. Genomes, variation and annotation

### 2.1 Dog10K ✅

- **Meadows et al. 2023, *Genome Biology* 24:187**: 20× data from **1,987 individuals** — **1,611 dogs (321 breeds)**, **309 village dogs**, **63 wolves**, **4 coyotes**; **>48 million** SNVs/indels/SVs across autosomes, X and mitochondria; >75% of variation discovered for 239 breeds; 94.9% of breeds form monophyletic clusters in 25 major clades; average breed dog differs from **UU_Cfam_GSD_1.0** by 26,960 deletions and 14,034 insertions >50 bp (wolves ~14% more — ⚠️ **structural** variants specifically, not all variants [verifier note, matching the correction already made in `../04_dog_aging_biology`]); retrogene insertions from **926 parent genes**; SNVs annotated with **SnpEff** and **Zoonomia phyloP** constraint; assessed as an imputation reference panel ✅ [verifier-confirmed: every figure in this bullet reproduced from the full text] [5]. (An author correction exists [42].) [verifier-added] Useful detail for anyone rebuilding the pipeline: most analyses use a refined set of **1,929** samples (the same panel Sohrab 2026 imputes against), the reference was UU_Cfam_GSD_1.0 **plus three ROS_Cfam_1.0 chrY contigs**, variants are deposited in the **European Variation Archive under PRJEB62420**, and the final filtered call set is 27,878,361 autosomal + 847,128 chrX biallelic SNVs.
- **Buckley & Ostrander 2024, *Genome Research* 34(6):811–821** — review framing Dog10K vs Darwin's Ark vs DAP, and calling Dog10K the largest single public release of high-coverage canine WGS ✅ [6].
- **Dog10K database** (Zhou et al., *NAR* 53(D1):D939, 2024/2025; DOI 10.1093/nar/gkae928 [verifier-added]): integrates the 1,987 genomes (**52,963,969 SNVs** per Table 1 — but ⚠️ **the same paper's text says "34.6 million SNVs"**, so the two figures in the one paper disagree [verifier-added]; Table 1 also says **324** breeds where Meadows 2023 says 321), **8,565 de novo mutations from 643 dogs across 43 breeds** ✅, scRNA-seq (**105,057 hippocampal nuclei**; **74,067 leukocytes**) ✅, and **bulk RNA-seq of 30 blood samples across ages** ✅ (dogs aged 1–9 y, against 469 human individuals aged 8–73 y [verifier-added]); accessible without registration at dog10k.kiz.ac.cn ✅ ("can access information freely … without registration or log in"); article CC BY-**NC** ✅ [7].
- Ageing relevance: the variant substrate for scoring ageing-gene damage; the 30-sample age-series RNA-seq and hippocampal atlas are directly ageing-adjacent.
- **CanVAS** (Brundage, UW–Madison; bioRxiv, Apr 2026) harmonises 15 public datasets into **15,451 dogs**. It covers 375+ breeds plus village dogs, dingoes, wolves and coyotes. The data are on CanFam4, imputed against the Dog10K panel (1,929 dogs), giving **9.7 M variants**, and are released under **CC BY 4.0** (Zenodo record 19186944). Arguably the best open substrate for a canine variant-effect predictor (proposal #2). Details and citation: [`../03_canine_bio_ai/companies_and_research_groups.md`](../03_canine_bio_ai/companies_and_research_groups.md) §CanVAS 🟡 (preprint).   - GHR: P10912 (638 aa) vs Q9TU69 (638 aa) → **82.3%** (525/638 = 82.29%)
  - GH1: P01241 (217 aa) vs P33711 (216 aa) → **68.4%** (147/215 = 68.37%) (the ligand is the divergent part of the axis; the receptor is nearly identical)
  - **Reconciliation with `../01_alphafold`, which reports IGF1R at 98.1%** [verifier-added]: both are correct and measure different things. **97.4% is the full-length precursor** comparison; **98.1% is the mature receptor** — human P08069 residues 31–1367 against dog A0A8I3RQ77 from residue 89 (the conserved `CGPGIDIRND` start), which I recomputed as **1,312/1,337 = 98.13%**. The gap between the two numbers is almost entirely the dog entry's **~60-residue N-terminal extension**, which is probably a gene-model artefact: of 110 differing alignment columns, 86 are N-terminal gaps and only **24 are genuine substitutions beyond human position 30**. For design work, quote 98.1% (mature) and start the dog chain at residue 89.
  - Implication: human-trained structure/variant/binder models transfer well to canine **IGF1R/IGF1**, less well to **GH1**; species-specific design is needed for GH itself.
- **IMGT canine immunogenetics**: Kustro Garnica et al. 2026 (*NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics*) biocurated the **IGK locus across nine canine assemblies**, annotating **7 breeds** (Bernese Mountain Dog, Boxer, Cairn Terrier, Labrador, Great Dane, Basenji, German Shepherd) into **40 genes and 97 alleles**, and documented CanFam3.1 artefacts ✅ [49]. This is the closest thing to a canine AIRR germline reference; **no large public canine BCR/TCR repertoire dataset was found** ❓.

### 2.4 Mendelian/variant knowledge bases

- **OMIA** (Online Mendelian Inheritance in Animals, University of Sydney): as displayed on 2026-10-04 — **6,084 phenes across species**; **dog: 1,019 traits**, **597 known likely causal variants**, **347 single-gene diseases with ≥1 known likely causal variant** ✅ [51]. Free for >30 years; **explicit licence not stated on the site** ❓.
- **iDog** (Tang et al. 2019, *NAR*): integrated dog/wild-canid resource — assemblies (dhole, wolf), variants from hundreds of WGS, curated phenotypes/diseases, RNA-seq expression, homologues, literature; bigd.big.ac.cn/idog ✅ [52].
- **Canine gene-constraint map** (Buckley et al. 2025, *Genome Research*): coding variation from **>2,000 dogs**, constraint computed from a trinucleotide-substitution mutation model; found a *PDGFRA* splice-acceptor variant (bifid nose in Çatalburun dogs) and **two new *LCORL* body-size variants** (frameshift in >25 kg breeds; intronic substitution in <10 kg breeds); breed-differentiation signatures are *depleted* of constrained genes ✅ [53]. This is the canine analogue of human gnomAD constraint and is the right prior for ageing-gene variant prioritisation.
- **Deleterious-load / breed-formation genetics**: Axelsson et al. 2021 *PLoS Genet* (deleterious variation accumulation; MMVD fixation in Cavalier King Charles Spaniels) [54]; Subramanian & Kumar 2024 (homozygous deleterious variant abundance vs breed morbidity) [55].
- **Body-size/ageing-axis variants**: *IGF1* small-size allele (Sutter et al. 2007 *Science*) [56]; the causal **IGF1-AS antisense lncRNA variant** identified from 1,431 genomes across 13 canid species (Plassais et al. 2022 *Current Biology*) [57]. *IGF1* alone controls ~15% of between-breed body-size variation [57].

## 5. Comparative-genomics and longevity reference data

- **Zoonomia** (Christmas et al. 2023, *Science*): whole-genome **Cactus alignment of 240 placental mammals** (241-way including two dog representatives 🟡) plus a protein-coding alignment of 427 mammals; **≥332 Mb (~10.7%)** of the human genome unusually constrained; **4,552 ultraconserved elements**; **101 million** significantly constrained single bases, **80% outside coding exons**, half with no ENCODE annotation ✅ [83]. Downloads: 240-species `.hal` alignment and phyloP conservation scores at `cglgenomics.ucsc.edu/data/cactus/`; **TOGA** ortholog annotations (GTF/BED12, referenced on human/mouse/chicken) at `genome.senckenberg.de/download/TOGA/`; 131 new assemblies via the project site; **no dog-referenced projection advertised** ✅; terms/privacy pages exist but a specific open licence was not stated ❓ [84].
- **Kowalczyk et al. 2020, *eLife* 9:e51089** (RERconverge): relative-evolutionary-rate method across the mammalian phylogeny treating absolute and relative longevity as quantitative traits; genes in **cell cycle, DNA repair, cell death, the IGF1 pathway and immunity** are under *increased* constraint in large/long-lived mammals; inflammation/DNA repair/NF-κB in species long-lived *for their size* ✅ [85]. Review update: Clark et al. 2025, *Annu Rev Genet* ✅ [86].
- **Tyshkovskiy et al. 2023, *Cell*** — multi-tissue RNA-seq across **41 mammalian species**; shared longevity mechanisms include **down-regulated *Igf1*** and up-regulated mitochondrial translation; identified KU-0063794 which extended mouse lifespan ✅ [87].
- **Tyshkovskiy et al. 2019, *Cell Metabolism*** — RNA-seq of mice under 8 longevity interventions, expanded to 17 with public data; hepatic longevity signatures; **GENtervention** app; predicted new interventions (chronic hypoxia, KU-0063794, ascorbyl-palmitate) ✅ [88].
- **Tyshkovskiy et al. 2026, *Nature*** — "Universal transcriptomic hallmarks of mammalian ageing and mortality": **>11,000 transcriptomes, >25 tissues, 4 mammals** (mouse, rat, macaque, human); conserved ageing signatures incl. **CDKN1A** and **LGALS3**; **module-specific clocks** (inflammation, interferon, mitochondrial, chromatin, ECM) showing that chronic disease accelerates the inflammatory module while caloric restriction/Klotho deficiency target metabolic modules ✅ [89]. **Dogs are not among the four species** — an obvious extension target.
- **Ma et al. 2016, *eLife*** — primary skin fibroblasts from **16 mammal species** under identical culture; longer-lived species up-regulate DNA repair and glucose metabolism; includes an ortholog-sequence pipeline ✅ [90].
- **AnAge / HAGR**: AnAge **Build 15** with **4,671 entries (4,645 species + 26 taxa)**; 4,141 longevity records; 2,454 ageing-process observations ✅. **The apparent date conflict is resolved** [verifier-corrected]: both pages say *Build 15*; the stats page's "last updated: December 16, 2024" is a page/statistics timestamp, while the entry page's "Build 15 (03/07/2023)" is the build's own release date — **there is no contradiction, and the build date is 03/07/2023** [91]. Dog entry: **maximum longevity 27 y (captivity)**, sample size "Large", data quality "Acceptable", longevity residual 109%, with the Bluey ~29 y record flagged as unverified ✅ [92]. HAGR also provides **GenAge** (307 human ageing genes 🟡 — the live HAGR home page says only "over 300"; 2,205 model-organism longevity genes ❓), **DrugAge** (1,097 compounds ❓ [verifier-downgraded]: the live page states "over 500 ageing-related drugs", so 1,097 needs a build-specific source), **GenDR** (214 genes ❓), **CellAge** (866 senescence genes ❓), **LongevityMap** (3,144 variants / 884 genes ❓) — [verifier note] the databases exist as named ✅, but of these counts only AnAge's were confirmed numerically [93]. "HAGR may be freely used for all purposes under some conditions" — not a standard open licence ❓.
- **Dog-specific ageing-rate phenotype**: Kraus, Pavard & Promislow 2013 (*Am Nat*) fitted mortality-hazard models to **74 dog breeds** and concluded large dogs die young mainly because of a **strong positive relationship between size and ageing rate**, not earlier senescence onset ✅ [94]. This is the quantitative target any molecular model should be asked to explain.

## 6. Canine cancer / comparative oncology (useful as the deepest canine molecular data)

- **Integrated Canine Data Commons (ICDC)**: NCI DCTD / Frederick National Laboratory, Gen3 on AWS, part of the Cancer Research Data Commons; portal **caninecommons.cancer.gov**. The CRDC repository page lists **12 distinct datasets** ✅ [verifier-confirmed by counting the study codes: COTC007B, COTC022, GLIOMA01, MGT01, NCATS-COP01, ORGANOIDS01, OSA01, OSA03, TCL01, UBC01, UBC02, UC01], six experimental strategies (clinical & biospecimen metadata JSON; RNA-seq; WGS; WXS; genotyping array; DNA methylation) ✅ and states **all data are open-access** and citable with attribution ✅ (verbatim: "All data in the ICDC are open-access and, with appropriate attribution, can be included in publications") [95]. **⚠️ Size question RESOLVED** [verifier-corrected]: the discrepancy was a stale snapshot, not an error. I queried the portal's own GraphQL API (`POST caninecommons.cancer.gov/v1/graphql/`) on 2026-10-04 and it returns **21 studies, 1,262 cases, 1,820 samples, 4,739 files, 5 programs** — so both the "12 datasets" (CRDC landing page, curated subset) and the 2024 "nine studies and 598 cases" [96] are outdated; **cite the live API**. [verifier note] ICDC's listed data types are **bulk** RNA-seq, WGS, WXS, genotyping and methylation — **there is no single-cell data in ICDC**. Example study: ICDC-Glioma with multi-omic data for 81 subjects 🟡 [97].
- **Comparative Oncology Trials Consortium (COTC)**: NCI-run network running trials in pet dogs with spontaneous cancers to inform human drug development (Gordon et al. 2009, *PLoS Med*; pharmacodynamic core 2010) ✅ [98][99]. I could not open the current Comparative Oncology Program page (403), so **current member-institution counts and trial numbers are unverified** ❓.
- **Canine cell lines / drug screens**: **Cellosaurus reports 990 cell lines for taxon 9615** ✅ [verifier-upgraded from 🟡: re-queried the Cellosaurus API in TSV mode and counted 990 accessions]. Gustafson et al. 2026 (*Vet Comp Oncol* 24(3):532–544) screened **12 targeted anticancer drugs across 13 canine and 6 human osteosarcoma cell lines** at clinically relevant exposures; four drugs (alisertib, crizotinib, onvansertib, sorafenib) were active and **responses were indistinguishable across species** ✅ [verifier-confirmed verbatim in the abstract] [100]. Leung et al. 2026 established canine B-cell-lymphoma PDX and cell-line models ✅ [101]. **There is no canine equivalent of GDSC/DepMap** — no large, uniformly processed canine cell-line × drug matrix was found ❓, which is a real bottleneck for in-silico screening validation.

1. **Darwin's Ark data availability** — **still open** ❓ [verifier]: the FAQ path in the original was wrong (it is `/faqs`, not `/faq`), but neither `/faqs`, `/collaborate` nor the *Science* data-availability statement yielded accession numbers or a licence.

3. **ICDC size** — **RESOLVED** [verifier]: the live GraphQL API returns 21 studies, 1,262 cases, 1,820 samples, 4,739 files, 5 programs (2026-10-04). The CRDC page's 12 datasets and the 2024 "nine studies/598 cases" are both stale snapshots.
4. **AnAge build date conflict** — **RESOLVED** [verifier]: no conflict. Both pages report Build 15; 03/07/2023 is the build date and 16 Dec 2024 is the stats-page update date.
5. **DAP low-pass sequencing details** (0.79× mean depth, 1,929-dog imputation panel, 29,089,701 SNPs, 9,871,985 common) — **RESOLVED and upgraded to ✅** [verifier]: all four figures read verbatim from the Sohrab 2026 bioRxiv full text (v2 JATS). The associated claim that these are "distributed on Terra as PLINK1 bfiles" is **not** supported and has been downgraded to ❓.
6. **Beagle near-T2T assembly** — **worse than 🟡** [verifier]: no Europe PMC record exists for it at all, so it is now ❓. **Grey wolf mCanLor1.2** — **RESOLVED and upgraded to ✅**: the data note (Sinding et al., *Wellcome Open Res* 6:310) confirms 2,447 Mb, 98.91% in 40 pseudomolecules, scaffold N50 66 Mb and BUSCO 95.5%.
7. **No pan-tissue canine cell atlas**, **no canine DepMap-style drug-response matrix**, **no large public canine immune-repertoire (BCR/TCR) dataset**, and **no canine eQTL atlas** were found ❓ — all four are genuine white spaces.
