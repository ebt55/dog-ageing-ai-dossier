# AI & Computational Tools Mapped onto Dog-Ageing Questions

> Part of the dog-ageing research dossier · Written 2026-10-04 by Opus 5.5 subagent · Status: independently verified by Fable 5.1 on 2026-10-04 — see ../07_verification/ai_toolkit_verification.md · Licence: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## TL;DR

- **Structure and design tools are essentially species-agnostic and therefore "dog-ready today"**: AlphaFold 3, Boltz-2, Chai-1, RFdiffusion/RFdiffusion3 (via Foundry), BindCraft and ProteinMPNN all take a sequence, so a canine IGF1R or GHR sequence from UniProt works immediately. The canine GH/IGF axis is also unusually favourable for transfer: I measured **IGF1R 97.4% human↔dog identity** (full-length precursor; **98.1% for the mature receptor** [verifier-added], which is the figure to quote for design work and the one used in `../01_alphafold`), **IGF1 precursor 91.9% (mature 70-aa IGF-1 100% identical)**, **GHR 82.3%**, but **GH1 only 68.4%** ✅ **independently recomputed by the verifier, all four values reproduced to the decimal** — so receptor-directed binders may transfer from human work, whereas the hormone itself needs dog-specific design.
- **Variant-effect models are the weakest link.** AlphaMissense releases **human-only precomputed scores and no weights** [1]; PrimateAI-3D is primate/human with an academic-licence download [2]; popEVE is explicitly human-cohort-conditioned [3]. The only published quantitative test of transferring these to animals (via a "mock human variant" strategy on OMIA variants) found **sensitivity 0.77–0.90 but specificity only 0.20–0.46 and auROC 0.55–0.61** ✅ [4]. Practical conclusion: use **ESM-2/ESM-1v/ESM C or GEMME/EVE run natively on canine sequences**, calibrated against canine gene constraint [5] and OMIA labels — not human precomputed scores.
- **DNA/regulatory models split sharply.** **Evo 2 has dog in its training corpus** (OpenGenome2 includes GCF_011100685.1 and Ensembl release-112 dog transcript/ncRNA sets) ✅ [verifier-confirmed by re-downloading and re-grepping `species_metadata.csv`] [6] and is Apache-2.0 with open weights ✅ [7] — making it **the most practical** frontier DNA model to run on canine sequence without retraining. ⚠️ [verifier-corrected] It is **not the only one**: InstaDeep's **NTv3** was pretrained on 9 Tbp of **the same OpenGenome2 corpus** (its own README: "Pretrained on 9 trillion base pairs from OpenGenome2 and post-trained on >16,000 functional tracks from 24 animal and plant species"), so dog sequence is in NTv3's pretraining too — though its checkpoints are **gated with licence "other"**, unlike Evo 2's Apache-2.0. **AlphaGenome is human + mouse only**, non-commercial API, no weights ✅ [8][9]; **Enformer/Borzoi are human/mouse** [10][11]; **GPN-Star** extended to five model organisms — *M. musculus*, *G. gallus*, *D. melanogaster*, *C. elegans*, *A. thaliana* — **not dog** ✅ [verifier-confirmed verbatim from the Nature paper] [12].
- **Single-cell "virtual cell" models are human-centric.** Geneformer and scGPT are human-only ✅ [13][14]; **UCE can embed species it never saw** ✅ [15]; **TranscriptFormer** covers **12 trained species** ✅ (TF-Metazoa, 112M cells: human, mouse, rabbit, chicken, African clawed frog, zebrafish, sea urchin, *C. elegans*, fruit fly, freshwater sponge + 2) with embeddings for 24 🟡 [verifier-downgraded: the 12-species training set and dog's absence are confirmed from the README's species table, but the "24 species with downloadable embeddings" count could not be reproduced from the current README] — **dog is in neither list** ✅, but the repo documents generating ESM-2 protein embeddings for new species, which is the concrete route to a canine-capable virtual cell ✅ [16]. State is trained on 167M cells ✅ under the **Arc Research Institute State Model Non-Commercial License** (code separately CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) ✅ [verifier-confirmed: both licence files read directly] [17]. Two 2025 benchmarks warn these models can be beaten by simple baselines, so treat them as representation tools, not oracles [18][19].
- **Ageing-specific AI is the most mature canine-ready layer**: human–dog dual-species methylation clocks (R = 0.97) ✅ [20], pan-mammalian universal clocks covering 185 species ✅ (11,754 arrays, 59 tissues, r > 0.96) [21], EnsembleAge for clock scoring ✅ (v0.99.1, MIT, "110+ individual clocks", Human/Mammal40k/Mammal320k platforms — all confirmed from the repo's README and DESCRIPTION) [22], DAP's 864-dog clock 🟡 (**still an unpublished preprint, and its mortality hazard rests on 49 deaths** [verifier-added]) [23], and transcriptomic longevity/mortality signatures from Gladyshev-lab work that explicitly implicate the **IGF1 axis** ✅ [24][25][26] — against which canine interventions can be scored in silico. ⚠️ [verifier note] A practical caveat for all of the array-based clocks: they are built on the **HorvathMammalMethylChip40** (~36k conserved CpGs), so they cannot be scored directly on RRBS or WGBS data without CpG-overlap mapping — which matters for any plan to score DAP's RRBS samples with the dual-species or universal clocks.
- **Biggest practical warning**: Serres Armero et al. 2024 showed canine *biological*-age clocks are heavily confounded by **breed population structure** and that biological-age signals "do not colocalize between datasets" ✅ [verifier-confirmed: the quote is verbatim — "methylation-related markers reflecting biological age signals are rare and do not colocalize between datasets" — as is "biological age methylation clocks are affected by population stratification and require heavy parameterization"; *Aging* 16(13):10724–10748] [27]. Any canine ageing-rate model must be ancestry-aware from the start.

## 2. Structure & design — what to actually build

**Why this is the most dog-ready layer.** None of AF3/Boltz-2/Chai-1/RFdiffusion/BindCraft/ProteinMPNN contains a species prior that excludes dog; they consume sequences and (optionally) MSAs. The practical blockers are licence terms (AF3 weights are non-commercial and must come from Google [29]) and **canine sequence hygiene**: UniProt has only **857 reviewed dog proteins out of ~46,500 entries**, with multiple ragged isoforms per gene (see `canine_data_resources.md` §2.3), so canonical-isoform selection is step zero.

**Measured transfer potential for the GH/IGF axis** ✅ (my own global alignments, BLOSUM62, gap −10/−0.5, from UniProt sequences):

| Protein | Human | Dog | Identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| IGF1R | P08069 (1367 aa) | A0A8I3RQ77 (1425 aa) | **97.4%** over 1,359 aligned columns |
| IGF1 precursor | P05019 | P33712 | **91.9%**; **mature 70-aa human IGF-1 is an exact substring of the dog precursor** |
| GHR | P10912 (638 aa) | Q9TU69 (638 aa) | **82.3%** |
| GH1 | P01241 | P33711 | **68.4%** |

Interpretation: a human-validated IGF1R-directed binder or antibody has a high prior probability of cross-reacting with dog; a human GH-directed reagent likely does not. Note also that **AlphaFold DB has dog IGF1 (pLDDT 55.19) and GHR (59.06) but returned 404 for the 1425-aa IGF1R entry** ✅ — i.e. for canine design work you should **fold your own canine structures** rather than rely on AFDB coverage [82].

**Concrete uses**
1. **Canine IGF-1/IGF1R interface modelling** with AF3 or Boltz-2 to find modulation sites; Boltz-2 adds an affinity head so small-molecule series can be ranked without docking software [30].
2. **Breed-variant structural triage**: take breed-enriched missense variants from Dog10K/DAP in ageing-relevant genes, fold wild-type vs variant with AF3/Chai-1, and combine ΔpLDDT/interface changes with ESM-2 log-likelihood deltas.
3. **Binder/decoy design** with BindCraft + ProteinMPNN + RFD3, using canine sequences, for IGF1R or GHR modulation as research reagents (then validated in canine cell lines — note there is no canine DepMap, so assays must be built).
4. **Antibody feasibility**: MAMMAL's fine-tuned antibody–antigen scores outperformed AF3 confidence on 5/7 antigens [62], so use MAMMAL as a ranking layer over structure-based candidates.

## 3. Variant effect prediction — the calibration problem

The honest state of play:

- **AlphaMissense**: Apache-2.0 code, **CC BY 4.0 precomputed predictions for human major transcripts and isoforms, and explicitly no released weights** ("we are not releasing the trained model weights… the code is not meant to be used for making new predictions") ✅ [verifier-confirmed: both quotes are verbatim in the current README, as are the Apache-2.0 code and CC BY 4.0 prediction licences] [1]. So you cannot run AlphaMissense on a dog protein; you can only look up the human orthologous position.
- **PrimateAI-3D**: scores downloadable for academic/non-profit use after signing a licence; commercial licensing through Illumina ✅ [verifier-confirmed from the repo README: "academic, non-profit research", a signed academic licence agreement, and `AI_licensing@illumina.com` for commercial use] [2]. Reported to outperform AlphaMissense in real-world cohorts [45].
- **The transfer penalty is measured**: Hu & Xie (2025) converted **284 "deleterious" and 90 "non-deleterious" OMIA variants from domestic mammals into mock human variants** and scored them with AlphaMissense, ESM-1b and PolyPhen-2: **sensitivity 0.77–0.90, specificity 0.20–0.46, auROC 0.55–0.61** ✅ [verifier-confirmed: every figure verbatim in the abstract; *Open Vet J* 15(10):5183–5191] [4]. That is near-useless discrimination, partly because animal "non-deleterious" variants include coat-colour-type functional changes (the authors' own explanation). [verifier note] Two scope clarifications: the quoted ranges are **across the three tools** (AlphaMissense 0.77/0.46; ESM-1b 0.86/0.32; PolyPhen-2 0.90/0.20), not the spread of any one tool; and the 374 variants span **eight species, of which 126 are dog** — no dog-specific performance figure is reported.
- **Therefore the defensible canine pipeline is native, not mapped**:
  1. Score canine missense variants with **ESM-2 / ESM-1v / ESM C** (masked-marginal or wt-marginal) directly on canine sequences [39][40][43].
  2. Add **GEMME** and/or protein-family-specific **EVE** models trained on canine-containing alignments for an independent evolutionary signal [46][47].
  3. Calibrate against **canine gene constraint** from the trinucleotide mutation model of Buckley et al. 2025 (>2,000 dogs) [5] — the canine analogue of gnomAD constraint — and against **OMIA's 597 dog causal variants / 347 characterised single-gene diseases** as positive labels [83].
  4. Use **Zoonomia phyloP** (already attached to Dog10K SNVs [84]) for non-coding and synonymous positions.
  5. **Build the missing artefact**: a canine popEVE analogue, substituting Dog10K/DAP allele presence for the UK Biobank cohort term [3]. This is the single highest-leverage missing tool in canine ageing genetics. ⚠️ [verifier note] **popEVE's repository is GPL-3.0** (confirmed from its LICENSE), so a derivative canine model inherits copyleft obligations — fine for an open public resource, a problem for closed downstream use. The upstream pieces it needs (EVE, ESM-1v) are MIT.

## 5. Single-cell / virtual-cell models

**Current canine compatibility**

- **UCE** is the only cell FM whose published claim includes embedding data from **species not in training** [15], and the Integrated Mega-scale Atlas covers 36M cells across 8 species — making it the lowest-friction way to put canine PBMC/lung/hippocampus atlases in a shared space with human/mouse ageing atlases.
- **TranscriptFormer**'s shipped species list (12 trained ✅, 24 with embeddings 🟡 [verifier: the 24 figure is not reproducible from the current README]) has **no dog** ✅ — but the repo explicitly documents generating protein embeddings for new species with ESM-2 ✅ (a "New Species" section: "new species that don't have pre-generated embeddings available for download") [16], which is the canonical path to a canine virtual cell. The repo is **MIT** ✅ (LICENSE.md, © 2025 CZI).
- **Geneformer** (human-only, V2 on ~104M transcriptomes [13][56]) and **scGPT** (33M human cells [14]) require orthologue renaming; dog→human one-to-one orthologues are available via Ensembl/TOGA, but gene-vocabulary mismatch degrades performance.
- **State** (SE embedding model trained on 167M cells; ST perturbation model) is the best-performing perturbation predictor published to date [17] but ships under a **non-commercial model licence** [59] — relevant if any downstream work is commercial.
- **Dog is absent from the CELLxGENE Census** (LTS 2025-11-08 lists human, mouse, macaque, marmoset, chimpanzee) ✅ [verifier-confirmed verbatim from the Census docs: "Census LTS release 2025-11-08 includes CELLxGENE Discover datasets from *Macaca mulatta*, *Callithrix jacchus*, and *Pan troglodytes* as well as *Homo sapiens* and *Mus musculus*"; the release-info page's per-species cell counts list exactly those five] [86], so canine data must be curated by hand.

**Mandatory caveats.** Ahlmann-Eltze, Huber & Anders (2025, *Nat Methods*) compared five foundation models and two other deep models against deliberately simple baselines for predicting post-perturbation transcriptomes and found **none outperformed the baselines** [18]. Kedzierska et al. (2025, *Genome Biology*) found zero-shot Geneformer/scGPT can be outperformed by simpler methods [19]. A 2026 calibration benchmark found classical logistic regression on matched genes matched or beat every frozen scGPT pipeline across six atlases 🟡 [87]. **Use cell FMs for representation/integration, and always report a linear baseline.**

**Data reality for canine fine-tuning** (see `canine_data_resources.md` §4): ~318,000 PBMC transcriptomes in one young-vs-senior beagle study [88], 105,057 hippocampal nuclei [89], 74,067 leukocytes [90], 68,323 PBMCs (leishmaniosis) [91], 30,040 PBMCs on canFam4 [92], 26,278 lung cells [93]. Summed, that is **well under a million canine cells, dominated by blood** — enough for adapter/LoRA-style fine-tuning and zero-shot transfer, not for pretraining.

### 6.1 Epigenetic clocks (most mature, directly canine)

| Clock | Basis | Why it matters for dogs |
|---|---|---|
| Thompson et al. 2017 | RRBS, 46 dogs + 62 wolves; 41/67/115-CpG clocks | only substantial **wolf** clock [94] |
| Wang et al. 2020 | 104 Labradors, syntenic-block capture bisulfite; `human_age = 16·ln(dog_age) + 31` | the dog↔human age translation function [95] |
| Horvath et al. 2022 | mammalian array, **742 samples / 93 breeds**; age and average-time-to-death clocks; **two human–dog dual-species clocks, R = 0.97**; EWAS of breed-weight↔lifespan ✅ all verbatim | the translational workhorse: an intervention that moves the dual clock in dogs has a defined human read-out. [verifier note] Its **top EWAS hits are *SF1* and *TTC8***, and the paper found **no** significant association of epigenetic age *acceleration* with breed weight or lifespan — so cite it for the clocks, not for an IGF-axis EWAS signal [20] |

| DAP 864-dog clock | >3M CpGs; largest size class ≈ **0.37 extra epigenetic years per chronological year**; **+34% mortality hazard per residual epigenetic year** (HR 1.34, 95% CI 1.06–1.69) 🟡 | the body-size→ageing-rate effect, measured epigenetically. ⚠️ [verifier-added] **Unpublished preprint (bioRxiv v2, Oct 2024) and only 49 deaths** behind the hazard estimate — treat as a lead, not a result [23] |
| Jin et al. 2024 | DNAm (R²=0.33) + **ATAC** (0.26) + combined (0.29) clocks in 71 dogs | second epigenetic modality; features near *BAIAP2*, *SCARF2* [68] |
| EnsembleAge (R) | 110+ clocks, auto platform detection (Human/Mammal40k/Mammal320), MIT | the practical scoring toolkit [22] |

### 6.2 Transcriptomic / multi-omic ageing signatures

- **Tyshkovskiy et al. 2019 (*Cell Metab*)**: RNA-seq of mice under 8 (then 17) longevity interventions; hepatic longevity signatures; **GENtervention** app; predicted chronic hypoxia, KU-0063794, ascorbyl-palmitate [24].
- **Tyshkovskiy et al. 2023 (*Cell*)**: multi-tissue RNA-seq across **41 mammalian species**; shared mechanisms include **down-regulated *Igf1*** and up-regulated mitochondrial translation; KU-0063794 then extended mouse lifespan [25].
- **Tyshkovskiy et al. 2026 (*Nature* 654(8117):173–188)**: **>11,000 transcriptomes, >25 tissues, 4 mammals (mouse, rat, macaque, human)**; universal ageing/mortality signatures incl. **CDKN1A** and **LGALS3** (confirmed at protein level against UK Biobank mortality/multimorbidity); **module-specific clocks** showing chronic disease accelerates the inflammatory module while caloric restriction/Klotho deficiency hit mitochondrial/metabolic modules ✅ [verifier-confirmed: the paper exists, and every element of this sentence — the counts, the four species, both gene names, the UK Biobank protein validation and the module behaviour — is verbatim in the abstract and article text] [26]. **Dog is not one of the four species — adding it is a clean, high-value project.** ✅
- **Gross et al. 2026 (*Nat Aging* 6(7):1516–1531)**: network-medicine framework mapping **2,358 longevity genes** onto the human interactome, measuring proximity of **6,442 compounds** to each ageing hallmark, plus a transcription-based **pAGE** metric; explicitly falsifiable ✅ [verifier-confirmed: both counts and the pAGE metric are verbatim in the abstract] [71].
- **Ma et al. 2016 (*eLife*)**: 16-species fibroblast panel under identical culture; longer-lived species up-regulate DNA repair and glucose metabolism; includes an ortholog-sequence pipeline reusable for canids [97].
- **Canine-native signatures now exist**: 9 ageing-related blood cell populations and 9 conserved CD8⁺ T-cell senescence markers across 19 dogs with scRNA-seq + proteomics + metabolomics [98]; immune/metabolic ageing reference in beagles prioritising *PLA2G4A* and *IRF4* [88]; DAP metabolomic mortality biomarkers that correlate with human ones [99][100]; canine microbiome age model [101].

### 6.3 Commercial ageing-AI platforms

- **PandaOmics** (Insilico Medicine): AI target/biomarker discovery; published ALS and ageing/GBM dual-purpose target cases [72][102][103].
- **Precious1GPT** (ageing-clock development + feature importance, *Aging* 2023) [73]; **Precious2GPT** (multi-omics transformer + conditional diffusion for synthetic multi-species/tissue samples, *npj Aging* 2024) [74]; **Precious3GPT** — explicitly **multimodal, multi-species, multi-omics, multi-tissue** for ageing research and drug discovery (preprint 2024) 🟡 [75]. The multi-species framing makes P3GPT the nearest off-the-shelf model for "simulate a canine tissue at age X under drug Y", though **dog coverage is unverified** ❓.

### 6.4 Where the canine intervention read-outs already exist

TRIAD — the **Test of Rapamycin In Aging Dogs** — is a parallel-group, double-masked, randomised, placebo-controlled multicentre trial in healthy middle-aged DAP dogs, described by its authors as "the first rigorous test of a pharmacologic intervention against biological aging with lifespan and healthspan metrics as endpoints to be performed outside of the laboratory in any species" ✅ [verifier-confirmed: the quote is verbatim in the *GeroScience* 47(3):2851–2877 abstract] [104]. Any in-silico intervention-ranking work should be designed to feed candidates into this kind of trial architecture, and to use its endpoints (lifespan, healthspan metrics, cognition batteries [105]) as the ground truth.

## Open questions / gaps

1. **GEMME** — **still unresolved** ❓ [verifier]: `github.com/elodielaine/gemme` 404s, `lcqb.upmc.fr` was unreachable, and a search found no live mirror. The book chapter is real (*Methods Mol Biol*, pp. 105–122). Do not plan around GEMME until a current distribution point is found.

Residual risk is low but counsel should confirm for commercial use.
3. **Chai-2** availability — **RESOLVED** [verifier]: **no public release**. Announced 30 Jun 2025; access is an early-access programme for selected academic and industry partners under a "Responsible Deployment policy". No weights or API.
4. **Nucleotide Transformer's 850-species list** — **RESOLVED: dog is not in it** [verifier] (only *C. l. dingo* and Arctic fox). But **NTv3 was pretrained on OpenGenome2, which does contain the dog assembly** — so the "dog-ready DNA model" list is Evo 2 **and** NTv3 (the latter gated).
5. **Precious3GPT** — **partly resolved** [verifier]: the weights **are** genuinely public (`insilicomedicine/precious3-gpt` and `-multi-modal`, **CC-BY-NC-4.0**, so non-commercial only). **Dog coverage remains ❓** — every documented prompt example uses `<species>human</species>`, and no canine species token is shown.
6. **MAMMAL's gene-expression modality** is almost certainly human-trained; its transferability to canine expression is ❓ (cross-check with `../02_mammal`).
7. **Whether DAP's DUA permits using its data to train and release model weights** (as opposed to analysing in Terra) ❓ — this gates any "canine foundation model" plan.
8. **No canine perturbation dataset** (CRISPR or compound) exists at single-cell scale, so State/ST-style perturbation prediction cannot yet be validated in dog ❓.
9. **TxGemma weights are gated under Health AI Developer Foundations terms**; the CC BY 4.0 I initially saw applies to the arXiv paper, not the weights — flagged to avoid a licence error. ✅ [verifier-confirmed via the HF API: `license: other`, `license_name: health-ai-developer-foundations`, `gated: auto`.]

11. **New open item** [verifier-added]: the **Tahoe-100M composition figures** ("50 cell lines, 379 molecules, 3 doses") are not in the dataset card and appear to come from a secondary preprint. The dataset itself is **CC0-1.0** and is Vevo Therapeutics', not Arc's. Re-derive the counts before citing them.
12. **New open item** [verifier-added]: whether **dog is among NTv3's 24 post-training track species** is unknown; its checkpoints are gated, so the species list could not be read.

## Verification notes

> Independent verification pass by **Fable 5.1**, 2026-10-04. Full report: `../07_verification/ai_toolkit_verification.md`.

**Method.** Licences were checked by reading the actual artefacts, not summaries: raw `LICENSE`/`LICENSE.md`/`MODEL_LICENSE.md` files for 28 GitHub repositories, the Hugging Face model and dataset APIs for 16 models and 3 datasets, and the raw model cards and READMEs for AF3, AlphaMissense, AlphaGenome, Boltz, Chai, Evo 2, State, UCE, GPN, TranscriptFormer, RFdiffusion, PrimateAI-3D, EnsembleAge, NT/NTv3, TxGemma, ESM3/ESMC, Geneformer and Tahoe-100M. Every DOI in the source list was resolved through Crossref (one 404: Kang 2026, which Europe PMC does hold), and ~30 claims were checked against Europe PMC abstracts or full texts. The four human↔dog alignments were recomputed from UniProt FASTA with Biopython, and the mature-domain pLDDT values were recomputed from AlphaFold DB v6 confidence files.

**What held up.** All 106 citations exist, with correct journals, years and first authors — **no fabricated or mis-attributed reference was found**. Of the 15 licence claims the brief singled out, 14 were right as written (AF3 weights non-commercial-from-Google; Boltz-2 MIT code+weights incl. commercial; Chai-1 Apache-2.0 code+weights; Arc State non-commercial model licence + CC BY-NC-SA code; NT v2 CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0; TxGemma HAI-DEF terms and gating), and the fifteenth (Enformer) was simply upgraded from 🟡 to ✅. The flagship scientific claims — AlphaMissense's "no weights", the Hu & Xie transfer penalty, Horvath's R = 0.97, Lu's 185 species, GPN-Star's five organisms, Tyshkovskiy 2026's four species without dog, Serres Armero's "do not colocalize" quote, CELLxGENE's five-species Census, TranscriptFormer's dog-free species list — were all confirmed verbatim.

**Most important changes.**
1. **"Evo 2 is the only frontier DNA model that can run on canine sequence" is wrong.** NTv3 was pretrained on the same OpenGenome2 corpus. Evo 2 keeps the practical crown because it is Apache-2.0 while NTv3 is gated; a new NTv3 row was added, and the claim was narrowed in three places.
2. **Nucleotide Transformer v2's dog status resolved to "not included"** (only dingo and Arctic fox are in the 850).
3. **Chai-2 resolved**: partner-only early access, no weights or API.
4. **Tahoe-100M licence corrected** to CC0-1.0 (Vevo, not Arc), and its composition figures downgraded to ❓.
5. **Precious3GPT weights confirmed public but CC-BY-NC-4.0**; dog coverage still unverified.
6. **Robin confirmed Apache-2.0**; **Kosmos has no public repository** (its 79.4% accuracy figure stays 🟡).
7. **IGF1R's two identity figures reconciled** — 97.4% precursor vs 98.1% mature — with the dog entry's ~60-residue N-terminal extension identified as the cause.
8. **DAP's 864-dog clock downgraded in effect**: still an unpublished preprint, 49 deaths behind the mortality hazard.
9. **A new methodological caveat was added**: the mature canine clocks are array-based (~36k conserved CpGs), so they cannot be scored directly on DAP's RRBS data without an explicit mapping step.
10. **popEVE's GPL-3.0 copyleft** flagged for the "popEVE for dogs" plan; **Horvath 2022 flagged** as unsuitable evidence for an IGF-axis EWAS signal.

**Still unresolved:** GEMME's current repository and licence; dog coverage in Precious3GPT and in NTv3's 24 post-training track species; TranscriptFormer's "24 embedding species" count; Tahoe-100M's exact composition; MAMMAL's expression-head transferability; and whether the DAP DUA permits releasing model weights trained on its data.
